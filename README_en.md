# ⚖️ RUI — Legal Translation into Plain Language

[Leia em Português](README.md)

> Translating law for those who matter most: the citizen.

RUI is a product concept (mobile and web app) that translates legal jargon into plain language, giving ordinary citizens the right to understand their own legal cases. Developed and presented as a pitch at the **Dia T** event.

This repository documents the **ideation, research, and product conception** — it does not contain code, as this is a concept and prototyping project.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Legal jargon is a real barrier to accessing justice. Technical terms make legal proceedings incomprehensible to those outside the field.

Data behind the project:
- **41.4%** consider legal jargon a problem
- **73.3%** are dissatisfied with legal communication

---

## 💡 The Solution

A legal translation platform in plain language, with three main features:

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| 📖 **Interactive glossary** | Tap a word to see its meaning |
| 📝 **Section summaries** | Understand each part of the case |
| 🤖 **RUI Assistant (AI Chatbot)** | Ask questions in natural language |

**Pillars:** Accessibility · Autonomy · Clarity

---

## 👥 Target Audience

Citizens with ongoing legal cases who need to follow and understand their own case without relying exclusively on technical explanations.

---

## 📱 Format

Product designed for **Mobile App and Web**, with prototypes developed for both platforms covering the main screens: access/login, my cases, timeline, interactive glossary, section summary, and RUI assistant.

---

## 📊 Estimated Benefits

- **70%** increase in the client's understanding of the case
- **40%** reduction in recurring questions to the lawyer
- **30%** reduction in time spent on repetitive explanations
- **24h** continuous access to case information in plain language

---

## 🌍 Social Impact

The project aligns with **Target 16.3 of the UN Sustainable Development Goals (2015)**: promoting the rule of law and ensuring equal access to justice.
